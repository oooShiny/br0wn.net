PodQueue Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

PodQueue ("the app," "we," "our") is a podcast player and queue-management app for Android. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it's used, and what is (and isn't) shared with anyone else.

Summary: PodQueue does not require an account, does not collect analytics, does not show ads, and does not sell or share your data. Your subscriptions, listening history, and downloads are stored only on your device. The app does contact podcast directories and podcast hosting servers to find shows and play episodes — that's covered in detail below.

Information We Collect

Information stored only on your device

PodQueue stores the following locally, in the app's private storage. None of it is transmitted to us — we don't operate a server that the app reports back to.

  • Podcasts you subscribe to, and episode metadata (titles, descriptions, publish dates, artwork URLs) fetched from those podcasts' own feeds
  • Your queue order and listening history (playback position, completed episodes)
  • Downloaded episode audio files, stored in the app's app-specific storage area
  • App preferences: playback speed, skip-forward/back intervals, Wi-Fi-only download setting, light/dark theme
  • API credentials, if you choose to enter your own Podcast Index or Listen Notes API keys instead of the app's built-in ones

This data is removed when you uninstall the app, or when you clear individual subscriptions/downloads within the app.

Information sent to third-party services

To find and play podcasts, PodQueue communicates directly with the following third parties. These requests happen from your device; we don't proxy or log them ourselves.

  • Podcast Index (podcastindex.org) — receives your search terms when you search for podcasts to subscribe to. See the Podcast Index privacy policy.
  • Listen Notes (listennotes.com) — used for podcast discovery and recommendations, receiving search terms and, to generate recommendations, the podcasts you're subscribed to. See the Listen Notes privacy policy.
  • Podcast RSS feeds and episode hosting servers — when you subscribe to a show or play/download an episode, your device connects directly to that show's own hosting infrastructure (e.g. the podcast's RSS feed host and audio CDN) to retrieve the feed and audio file. These are operated by each individual podcast publisher, not by us.

As with any network request, these services will typically see your device's IP address and standard connection metadata as an inherent part of the request — this is not something PodQueue adds or configures beyond what's needed to make the request.

What we don't collect

  • No account registration, sign-in, name, or email address
  • No analytics or usage tracking SDKs
  • No advertising or ad-identifier usage
  • No crash-reporting service
  • No access to contacts, camera, microphone, or location

Permissions the App Requests

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to search for, subscribe to, and download/stream podcasts
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK — to keep audio playing while the app is in the background, with a visible playback notification
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show the playback controls notification (Android 13+)
  • WAKE_LOCK — to prevent playback from being interrupted by the device sleeping

Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. The only data leaving your device goes directly to the podcast-directory and podcast-hosting services described above, as a direct consequence of the searches and playback actions you initiate.

Data Retention and Deletion

All app data lives locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by removing individual subscriptions/downloads in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android system settings, or uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

PodQueue is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since the app collects no personal information on our end, there is no PodQueue-held data to request the deletion of.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and post the revised version at this same URL.
